# Contributing and reporting problems

Thank you for trying these new sBitx features. This is an amateur project, so replies may take a few days.

## Reporting a problem
Open an issue and include what is in `BUG_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md`. The more of it you fill in, the faster it can be fixed.

## Suggesting a feature
Open an issue with the title starting "Idea:". Say what you want to do on the air and what is missing.

## Sending a change
1. Fork the repository and make your change on a branch.
2. Keep each change small and explain what it does and how you tested it.
3. Never test transmit changes on the air first. Use a dummy load.
4. Do not include other people's code unless its licence allows it. See `NOTICE.md`.

## Licence of contributions
By sending a change you agree that your contribution can be published under the MIT licence in `LICENSE`, as far as it
is your own work. Code taken from the original sBitx project stays under its own terms.
